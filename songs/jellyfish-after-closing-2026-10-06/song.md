# TITLE

Jellyfish After Closing

# STYLE

Indie pop / alternative R&B, 104 BPM, bittersweet, lucid, quietly triumphant, intimate alto vocal with conversational phrasing, rubbery synth bass, glassy electric piano, muted guitar, crisp drum machine, warm analog pads, verses close and nocturnal, chorus wide and luminous, polished late-night production with gently grainy cassette warmth.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
Your text says, “Aquarium. After close?”  
One line glowing through my cracked phone screen  
I put on the jacket you left at my place  
And take the last train downtown at 12:15  

You’re waiting by the employee-only doors  
Hands in your pockets, hair damp from the rain  
The lobby smells like saltwater and bleach  
Neither of us says the other’s name  

We walk past sharks asleep in blue  
Past fogged-up glass and the humming pumps  
Your shoulder almost touches mine  
Then both of us remember not to  

[Pre-Chorus]  
The last train makes its announcement  
Somewhere under the city floor  
You look at me like you’re still deciding  
What this night is for  

[Chorus]  
Under the jellyfish after closing  
Everything moves slow and bright  
You tell me you’re leaving in the morning  
I say, “I know,” though I don’t know why  

There’s a little blue light between us  
Pulsing like it wants to stay  
I could ask you to take me with you  
But I let the beautiful thing end this way  

[Verse 2]  
We sit beside the moon jelly tank  
Your knees drawn up against the rail  
You say the new job starts on Monday  
Three time zones and a rented place  

I ask if you’ve packed the coffee maker  
You laugh, then look down at your shoes  
The pumps keep turning in the walls  
Like the building has a pulse to lose  

Outside, the city thins to silence  
Wet black tile beneath our feet  
You touch the scar beside my thumb  
Like you’re checking if I’m still me  

[Pre-Chorus]  
The first gray buses start to gather  
Behind the loading bay  
You say, “I thought you might be angry”  
I say, “I was yesterday”  

[Chorus]  
Under the jellyfish after closing  
Everything moves slow and bright  
You tell me you’re leaving in the morning  
I say, “I know,” though I don’t know why  

There’s a little blue light between us  
Pulsing like it wants to stay  
I could ask you to take me with you  
But I let the beautiful thing end this way  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Bridge]  
At the gift shop, I buy a keychain  
Tiny plastic moon in a paper bag  
You say, “You could come, you know”  
So quietly I almost miss the chance  

My phone is warm against my palm  
The screen is cracked across your face  
I open up a voice memo  
And give the silence somewhere to go  

“Thank you for the years we had  
Even the ones we couldn’t save  
I loved you past the point of holding  
I’m letting love become a doorway”  

[Final Chorus]  
Under the jellyfish after closing  
Everything moves slow and bright  
You’re leaving with the morning buses  
I’m learning not to call it mine  

There’s a little blue light between us  
Still pulsing as you walk away  
I could ask you to take me with you  
But I let the beautiful thing end this way  

Jellyfish after closing  
Blue fire fading into gray  
I leave through the employee exit  
With your last night glowing in my hand
