# TITLE

Key in the Lint Trap

# STYLE

Indie pop, 112 BPM, bittersweet and quietly triumphant, intimate conversational lead vocal, chiming electric guitar, Wurlitzer, warm synth bass, acoustic drums, handclaps, verses close and chorus wide, glossy cassette grain with bright room reverb

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
It’s 2:13, red numbers on the wall  
Rain draws crooked lines across the glass  
I’m washing what I carried from the old place  
You’re folding quarters by the change machine  
Like we just happen to be strangers here  
Like this isn’t your favorite laundromat  

You say, “How’ve you been?” I say, “Keeping busy”  
The dryer turns a blue sock inside out  
There’s detergent in the cuffs of my old jacket  
And one more thing we haven’t talked about  

[Pre-Chorus]  
You ask if I still have it  
I ask if you still do  
The little silver answer  
To a door we’re both through  

[Chorus]  
Maybe closure’s in the lint trap  
Under everything we leave behind  
Not in the words you never gave me  
Not in changing either mind  
Let the quarters line the table  
Let the rain erase the street  
If I walk out clean tomorrow  
That’s enough for me  

[Verse 2]  
The machine eats one of your quarters  
You tap the glass like it can hear  
You say, “I thought you’d bring the spare key”  
I say, “I thought you’d want it back this year”  

The dryers thump against the rainy morning  
The fluorescent lights begin to fade  
You tell me that the rent went up in April  
I tell you that the coffee’s still too late  

And somewhere underneath the small talk  
The question loses all its shape  
No courtroom, no dramatic ending  
Just warm clothes and a little break  

[Pre-Chorus]  
You ask if I resent you  
I watch the blue sock spin  
The room keeps turning softly  
And neither of us wins  

[Chorus]  
Maybe closure’s in the lint trap  
Under everything we leave behind  
Not in the words you never gave me  
Not in changing either mind  
Let the quarters line the table  
Let the rain erase the street  
If I walk out clean tomorrow  
That’s enough for me  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Bridge]  
The dryer stops with a tired little shudder  
You pull the door, I reach inside  
There it is beside the blue sock  
Silver, stupid, warm, and mine  

I hold it up beneath the buzzing  
You laugh once, then look away  
All this time it wasn’t in your keeping  
It was waiting where the dust collects  

I put it in the pocket of my clean shirt  
No speech arrives, and none is due  
The sky turns silver in the windows  
And I don’t need to come back through  

[Final Chorus]  
I found the key in the lint trap  
Under everything we leave behind  
Not in the words you never gave me  
Not in changing either mind  
Let the quarters line the table  
Let the rain erase the street  
I walk out clean into the morning  
With the whole day under me  

I found the key in the lint trap  
Blue sock tumbling, windows bright  
You can keep the old apartment  
I’ve got nothing there tonight  
Let the quarters line the table  
Let the last machine repeat  
I walk out clean into the morning  
And the door stays where it’s meant to be
