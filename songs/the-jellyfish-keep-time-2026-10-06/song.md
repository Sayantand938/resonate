# TITLE

The Jellyfish Keep Time

# STYLE

Indie pop / alt-R&B, 108 BPM, bittersweet, buoyant, quietly triumphant, intimate conversational lead vocal, rubbery synth bass, glassy electric piano, muted nylon-string guitar, crisp live drums, celesta, verses spare and chorus wider, polished nocturnal production with soft fluorescent grain and gentle low-mid warmth.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
It’s 2:07, I’m wringing out the mop  
Blue light breaking on the tile  
The pumps keep humming through the dark  
Like they’ve been up here all the while  

Your voice note waits beneath the keys  
I press play, then watch the glass  
You say, “Can we meet before sunrise?”  
Like the years between us passed  

The jellyfish rise, the jellyfish fall  
Soft as a thought I can’t quite hold  
Every pulse in the blue-lit wall  
Says nothing stays the way it’s told

In my locker, your invitation  
Gets damp around the edges  
I keep my hand above the name

The jellyfish keep time, keep time  
And I’m still here when the dark turns white
