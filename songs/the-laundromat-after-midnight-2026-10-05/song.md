# TITLE

The Laundromat After Midnight

# STYLE

Indie pop, 112 BPM, restless, tender, quietly hopeful, intimate conversational lead vocal, chorusy electric guitar, warm analog synth, melodic electric bass, crisp acoustic drums, subtle piano, verses sparse and close, chorus wider and lifting, polished bedroom-pop texture with fluorescent-night shimmer and gently compressed vocals.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
It’s 2:17 in late November  
Rain draws lines down the window glass  
The ceiling lights keep buzzing overhead  
Like they don’t know the night has passed  

Your denim jacket’s heavy with water  
One sleeve twisted inside out  
I feed the coins into the washer  
And watch the blue street flicker out  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Then something hard hits the metal drum  
A little paper folded twice  
I pull it from the pocket  
And read the date beneath the lights  

[Chorus]  
I’m not forgetting, I’m learning the shape  
Of a life with your shadow not taking the space  
The dryers keep turning, the whole room shines  
I can carry what happened and still take the first bus I find  

I don’t have to chase you to prove I still care  
I can leave your name folded somewhere  
Between what was ours and the morning sky  
I can carry the memory and still choose my life  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Verse 2]

Two seats from the back, one-way fare  
I picture you beside the station  
Rain in your hair, your suitcase there  

The sign above the change machine is blinking  
A bus sighs low along the street  
For half a second, I’m already running  
With your jacket hanging from my sleeve  

[Pre-Chorus]  
But the washer clicks into silence  
And the last spin slows down  
I hear my own breath in the quiet  
Like a door unlocking now  

[Chorus]

[Bridge]  
I take the warm clothes from the dryer  
Fold the sleeves, smooth out the seams  
Put the ticket in the lost-and-found box  
With the jacket, the shirt, and the in-between  

No grand goodbye, no final answer  
Just wet pavement turning silver-blue  
I miss you more than I expected  
But missing you is not a road I have to follow through

Of a life with your shadow not taking the space
