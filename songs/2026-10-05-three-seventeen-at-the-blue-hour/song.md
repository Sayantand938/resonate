# TITLE

Three-Seventeen at the Blue Hour

# STYLE

Indie pop / alt-R&B at 104 BPM, tender restless hopeful, intimate conversational lead vocal with a bruised upper register, rubbery synth bass, clean electric guitar, Rhodes piano, crisp drum kit, muted trumpet, verses close and chorus wide, glossy nocturnal production with rain-slick shimmer.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
Three-seventeen, my phone lights up  
Rainwater running down the glass  
Your voice says, “I’m still working nights  
If you’re awake, come by, just ask”  

There’s a pause beneath the traffic hum  
Like you’re standing somewhere small  
Then, “I don’t know what I’m supposed to say  
I just wanted you to call”  

I pull my jacket from the chair  
Find the bike key in the dark  
The city’s empty at this hour  
Except the red lights changing guards  

[Pre-Chorus]  
And every block brings back the years  
Your name blinking unanswered  
All those little silences  
Still ringing in the handset  

[Chorus]  
So I’m riding through the blue hour  
Spokes throwing streetlight gold  
To the diner with the blue sign  
Where you said you’d be alone  
You don’t have to fix the whole thing  
I don’t need a perfect truth  
Just stay until the coffee cools  
Just let me sit with you  

[Verse 2]  
The bell above the door gives out  
A tired little silver sound  
You’re behind the counter pouring  
Steam against the window now  

You look older than the picture  
I kept folded in a drawer  
You say, “I had no speech prepared  
I only wanted to see you before four”  

There’s toast on two untouched plates  
Butter softening at the side  
You ask me how the job is going  
I say, “Fine,” and then I lie  

[Pre-Chorus]  
You don’t reach across the table  
I don’t make you earn my hand  
We let the silence have a chair  
And try to understand  

[Chorus]  
We’re sitting through the blue hour  
Under letters humming bright  
With the rain along the windows  
And the trucks along the highway lights  
You don’t have to fix the whole thing  
I don’t need a perfect truth  
Just stay until the coffee cools  
Just let me sit with you  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Bridge]  
You say, “I missed more than I knew  
I thought time would make it plain”  
I say, “I kept expecting you  
To call and say my name”  

The morning starts behind the glass  
A pale line over the road  
You write your number on a napkin  
Like I never used to know it  

Then you say, “Next Sunday, noon?”  
I say, “Yeah, I can do that”  
No promise big enough to break  
Just a time and a place to start  

[Final Chorus]  
We’re leaving in the blue hour  
Though the sign is turning white  
Two plates gone cold behind us  
Two shadows stepping into light  
We don’t have to fix the whole thing  
We can take it day by day  
But you showed up before the morning  
And I showed up anyway  

Three-seventeen at the blue hour  
I can still hear what you said  
Not “forgive me,” not “I’m sorry”  
Just, “I’m here—are you awake?”  

We don’t have to fix the whole thing  
We can take it day by day  
There’s a Sunday on the calendar  
And a reason not to fade  

[Instrumental Outro]
