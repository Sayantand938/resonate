# TITLE

Receipts in the Rain

# STYLE

Indie pop with alt-R&B inflections, 104 BPM, tender, restless, quietly triumphant, intimate conversational lead vocal, electric piano, muted electric guitar, rounded synth bass, crisp drums, subtle string pad, verses sparse and chorus wider, glossy late-night texture with softly saturated vocal layers.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
I lock the grocery store at 2:13,  
fluorescent aisles go dark behind me.  
My jacket’s been hanging by the staff-room door,  
and something stiff slides onto the floor.  

A year-old receipt, the ink gone gray,  
three bruised bananas, detergent, takeaway.  
Your number still lives in the pocket fold—  
a little white witness to the night getting cold.  

I catch the last bus under pharmacy blue,  
fog on the glass, the whole town moving through.  
I read every item like a list of mistakes,  
and practice the sorry my mouth never says.

[Pre-Chorus]  
I could type it out,  
I could watch it turn blue,  
but the city keeps rolling  
like it knows what to do.

[Chorus]  
Receipts in the rain, I let them go,  
all of the words I was scared to show.  
Your number stays quiet, my hands stay clean,  
I’m learning to forgive the person I’ve been.  
Receipts in the rain, break in the street—  
one little ending under my feet.  
I don’t need your answer to know what is true:  
I can leave this night without leaving me too.

[Verse 2]  
The bus lets me out by the old laundromat,  
shuttered and silver, the sign hanging flat.  
That’s where we met with a pocket of change,  
you laughed when the dryer swallowed your change.  

I pass the deli where I bought that coffee,  
the pharmacy window still glowing softly.  
Every red light throws your face on the road,  
then the tires move on and the picture lets go.  

At the corner, I turn the receipt in my hand,  
the paper is soft from the damp in the air.  
I see “mint gum,” “black coffee,” “one detergent,”  
small proof we were real, not proof I was urgent.

[Pre-Chorus]  
I could call you now,  
say the perfect thing,  
but the rain on the awning  
sounds better than ringing.

[Chorus]  
Receipts in the rain, I let them go,  
all of the words I was scared to show.  
Your number stays quiet, my hands stay clean,  
I’m learning to forgive the person I’ve been.  
Receipts in the rain, break in the street—  
one little ending under my feet.  
I don’t need your answer to know what is true:  
I can leave this night without leaving me too.

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Bridge]  
So I tear it once by the laundromat door,  
then once more for the things I rehearsed before.  
The pieces stick briefly to both of my hands,  
then wash down the curb like they understand.  

No grand last message, no carefully staged goodbye,  
just wet little fragments and a paling sky.  
The bus pulls away with a tired blue glow—  
I wave to the driver, though he’ll never know.

[Final Chorus]  
Receipts in the rain, I let them go,  
all of the words I was scared to show.  
Your number stays quiet, my hands stay clean,  
I’m learning to forgive the person I’ve been.  

Receipts in the rain, gone with the street,  
a year of old weather dissolving beneath.  
I don’t need your answer, I know what is true:  
I can leave this night, and come home as me too.  

Receipts in the rain, receipts in the rain—  
I make coffee for the driver, then walk through the day.  
Your number stays quiet, the morning comes through—  
I can leave this night without losing me too.

[Instrumental Outro]
