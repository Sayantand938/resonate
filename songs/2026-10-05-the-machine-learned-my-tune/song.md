# TITLE

The Machine Learned My Tune

# STYLE

Indie pop with electronic folk accents, 108 BPM, curious and hopeful, intimate clear lead vocal with slightly breathless delivery, warm electric piano, plucked acoustic guitar, soft analog synth, melodic bass, restrained live drums, verses close and chorus wider, polished lo-fi texture with gentle tape saturation.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
It’s 2:17, the apartment’s holding still  
Blue light on the ceiling, rain against the sill  
Cold coffee by my elbow, bitter as the night  
A cheap keyboard breathing through a cable held too tight  

I type the little melody in letters, bars, and code  
A line of plain text carrying a secret load  
The terminal keeps blinking like it knows what I’m afraid of  
I press enter, hear the speakers crackle—  
Then the tune falls out of shape

And try the line again

And I’m learning it too

Find its way through
