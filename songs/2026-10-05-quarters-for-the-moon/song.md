# TITLE

Quarters for the Moon

# STYLE

Upbeat indie pop with soulful R&B inflections, 112 BPM, restless, tender, hopeful, intimate conversational lead vocal, electric piano, muted guitar, warm synth bass, crisp drum kit, glockenspiel, verses close and chorus wider, polished nocturnal production with soft tape saturation and fluorescent shimmer.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
2:17, rain on the glass,  
the whole town sleeping in the laundromat.  
You’re folding shirts beneath the buzzing light,  
one little baby shirt, set carefully aside.  

I’ve got my phone facedown by the change machine,  
three missed calls glowing blue and green.  
You ask if I’ve got quarters for the dryer.  
I say, “Only if you promise not to ask why I’m here.”  

[Pre-Chorus]  
The washers turn like silver planets,  
round and round, but going nowhere.  
You pour two coins into my paper cup,  
like we’ve already made it somewhere.  

[Chorus]  
We’ve got quarters for the moon,  
loose change and a little room.  
Enough to keep the night in motion,  
enough to make one honest move.  
You don’t have to tell me everything,  
I don’t have to tell you who I’ve been.  
Just keep the door from closing too soon—  
we’ve got quarters for the moon.  

[Verse 2]  
The coffee from the vending machine tastes burnt,  
you take one sip and say, “That’s what it’s worth.”  
I give you mine, you give me half your truth:  
you’re leaving on the first bus when the sky turns blue.  

I say I’m only waiting out the rain.  
You say, “That’s funny. I was gonna say the same.”  
Your phone lights up beside the folding chair;  
you watch it ring until the room goes quiet again.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
The dryers thump like tired hearts,  
the red lights blink in rows.  
You fold that tiny shirt once more  
and tuck it underneath your clothes.  

[Chorus]  
We’ve got quarters for the moon,  
loose change and a little room.  
Enough to keep the night in motion,  
enough to make one honest move.  
You don’t have to tell me everything,  
I don’t have to tell you who I’ve been.  
Just keep the door from closing too soon—  
we’ve got quarters for the moon.  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Bridge]  
At 4:06, the machines go still.  
The silence says what neither of us will.  
I type three words, then I let them go:  
“I’m not coming home.”  

You shoulder your bag by the rain-striped door.  
I say, “The bus station’s fifteen minutes more.”  
You look at me like I’ve changed the plan.  
I say, “Maybe. Get in if you can.”  

[Final Chorus]  
We’ve got quarters for the moon,  
loose change and a little room.  
The town is dark, but the road is open,  
and I can finally feel it move.  
You don’t have to tell me everything,  
I don’t have to promise anything.  
Just keep the door from closing too soon—  
we’ve got quarters for the moon.  

4:40 beneath the station clock,  
your bus is humming, my engine’s hot.  
No kiss, no vow, no reason to pretend—  
just two people choosing where the road begins.  

We’ve got quarters for the moon,  
and morning coming through.  
You take your bag, I take the long way home—  
but I don’t go back to the life I knew.  

[Instrumental Outro]
