# TITLE

The Blue Plastic Chair

# STYLE

Nocturnal indie pop, 108 BPM, tender, restless, quietly final, intimate conversational lead vocal, chorus-treated electric guitar, warm analog synth, melodic electric bass, brushed live drums, muted piano, verses sparse with a widening final chorus, polished bedroom-pop with fluorescent late-night warmth and lightly saturated tape texture.
