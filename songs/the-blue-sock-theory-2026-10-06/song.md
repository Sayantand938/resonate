# TITLE

The Blue Sock Theory

# STYLE

Indie pop with funk-pop and soft R&B inflections, 112 BPM, playful, tender, quietly hopeful, intimate conversational lead vocal, muted electric guitar, warm analog synth, rubbery electric bass, crisp live drums, glockenspiel, verses lean and chorus buoyant, lightly tape-worn warmth with glossy fluorescent shimmer.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
Thursday, 2:17, rain ticks hard against the glass  
I’m counting quarters, wiping soap from washers four and five  
The vending coffee tastes like cardboard and burnt sugar  
Then there it is again—  
One bright blue sock inside the dryer  

Under the lint screen, folded small  
A joke on paper thin as skin:  
“Why did the sock cross the road?  
It was tired of getting left behind.”  
I laugh alone, then write beneath it  
“Mine got farther than yours.”

[Pre-Chorus]  
Receipt paper, crooked letters  
Tucked where only I would see  
Somebody out there knows this hour  
Somebody’s leaving part of them with me  

[Chorus]  
Call it the blue sock theory  
One little thing can keep you here  
When the night runs cold and empty  
A small blue light appears  
If you leave a note, I’ll leave one back  
If you disappear, I’ll wait  
Maybe nobody’s lost forever  
Maybe we just miss the train  

[Verse 2]  
By week three, I know the rhythm  
Dryer twelve, then coffee, then the joke  
“Why was the washing machine so calm?  
It had a lot of inner spin.”  
I answer, “That one’s terrible,”  
Then smile before I close the door  

I tell them I used to take the long way home  
Past the river, past the loading cranes  
Now I clock out, lock the change machine  
And watch the blue sock turn  
Like a tiny flag in heavy weather  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Lint-screen dust on both our secrets  
Ink gone soft from steam and heat  
I don’t know your name or where you’re sleeping  
Still your handwriting changes me  

[Chorus]  
Call it the blue sock theory  
One little thing can keep you here  
When the night runs cold and empty  
A small blue light appears  
If you leave a note, I’ll leave one back  
If you disappear, I’ll wait  
Maybe nobody’s lost forever  
Maybe we just miss the train  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Bridge]  
Then Thursday comes with no blue cotton  
No bad joke, no folded clue  
Just a note beneath the lint screen:  
“Had to leave. My mother’s sick.  
Thank you for answering. I needed proof  
That the world could still answer too.”  

I read it twice beside the washer  
Rain is running silver down the street  
At sunrise, I hang every blue sock  
In a row across the window  
Like a flag for someone I’m ready to meet  

[Final Chorus]  
Call it the blue sock theory  
One little thing can bring you back  
Through the glass, I see you standing  
In a coat still wet and black  
You lift your hand, I lift the door  
The whole bright room begins to shake  
Maybe nobody’s lost forever  
Maybe we just need one more day  

Call it the blue sock theory  
You laugh, and I can hear it clear  
All this time, I thought I worked here  
Turns out I was waiting here  
If you leave a note, I’ll leave one back  
If you stay, I’ll pour the coffee  
Maybe nobody’s lost forever  
Maybe we can start in person  

[Instrumental Outro]
