# TITLE

The Aquarium Closes at Midnight

# STYLE

Indie pop with subtle R&B inflections, 104 BPM, tender, nocturnal, quietly triumphant, intimate clear lead vocal with restrained ache, electric piano, muted guitar, warm synth bass, crisp drum kit, glassy mallet tones, verses close and sparse, final chorus luminous, polished wet reflective textures.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
It’s 11:47 in the blue gallery  
I’m pushing rainwater toward the drain  
Your black hair tie is looped around the bench  
Like you might come back for it again  

There’s your name scratched under the whale display  
Half a heart, then the blade gave out  
I still keep your voicemail unopened  
Three seconds of you breathing before you hang up  

The pumps hum low beneath my shoes  
The whole place shimmers when the lights go dim  
I talk to you without my mouth  
Like the fish might carry it to where you live  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Tonight your box is waiting by the door  
I’ve made the room look like before  
But something in me finally knows  
A house can hold what I let go  

[Chorus]  
The aquarium closes at midnight  
The blue goes dark across the tile  
You don’t have to find your way back  
I can make a life that glows awhile  

There’s an empty cardboard box by the doorway  
There’s a little more air in every room  
The aquarium closes at midnight  
And I’m not waiting here for you  

[Verse 2]  
I line up our old cups beside the sink

I say, “I just have something to pass”
