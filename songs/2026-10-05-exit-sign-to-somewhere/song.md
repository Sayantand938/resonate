# TITLE

Exit Sign to Somewhere

# STYLE

Dreamy contemporary R&B with subtle pop crossover, 94 BPM, restless, hopeful, weightless; intimate velvet-toned lead vocal with close harmonies; Rhodes piano, muted electric guitar, warm sub bass, crisp programmed drums, glassy synths; verses restrained, chorus wide and memorable; polished late-night production with soft tape saturation.
