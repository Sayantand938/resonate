# TITLE

Quarters in the Rain

# STYLE

Indie pop / alternative R&B, 96 BPM, nocturnal, tender, quietly triumphant, intimate conversational alto lead vocal, muted electric guitar, warm synth bass, Rhodes piano, crisp drum machine, cello, verses sparse and chorus wider, polished grainy late-night texture with organic human room

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
2:17 on the laundromat clock  
Rain drawing veins down the window glass  
You’re by the change machine with a broken wheel  
Holding your suitcase like it might hold fast  

Wet denim breathing detergent steam  
Blue vending light across the tile  
You look up once, then look away  
Like three lost years could fit inside a mile  

I take the empty chair beside your load  
The washers hum where we don’t speak  
Traffic hisses through the flooded street  
Your hands are colder than they should be  

[Pre-Chorus]  
You count the quarters, I count the cracks  
Neither of us knows how to turn back  
Then your coat slips open, and there it is—  
A red house key on a silver ring  

[Chorus]  
We don’t say sorry, we say, “Is it dry?”  
We watch the warm clothes turn behind the glass  
You give me half the bench, I give you time  
Like maybe that’s enough to start again at last  

With quarters clicking in the rain  
With your suitcase leaning into mine  
No grand return, no perfect line  
Just leave the light on, I’ll walk you home tonight  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Verse 2]  
The dryer stops with a tired little chime  
You pull the door, but the denim’s damp  
Your old black sweater holds the smell  
Of every room we never talked back in  

I shake my pocket—three coins, then one  
The last of what I brought that night  
I feed them in, and the machine wakes up  
A small blue circle burning bright  

You say, “I kept it all this time.”  
I know before you show me what  
The red key rests inside your palm  
Warm from the place you never shut  

[Pre-Chorus]  
I don’t ask why you came this far  
You don’t ask if I still know the yard  
The dryer turns, the window shines  
Rainwater running over fluorescent lines  

[Chorus]  
We don’t say sorry, we say, “Is it dry?”  
We watch the warm clothes turn behind the glass  
You give me half the bench, I give you time  
Like maybe that’s enough to start again at last  

With quarters clicking in the rain  
With your suitcase leaning into mine  
No grand return, no perfect line  
Just leave the light on, I’ll walk you home tonight  

[Bridge]  
At 4:41, the rain lets up  
The town is gray and almost still  
You test the cracked wheel on the pavement  
It catches, then it rolls uphill  

I take the handle without asking  
You let it go without a fight  
The key makes a small sound in your pocket  
Like a door remembering the right  

[Final Chorus]  
We never say sorry—not tonight  
We say, “The clothes are warm,” and step outside  
You take the left, I take the right  
Then we meet again beneath the porch light  

With quarters clicking in the rain  
With your suitcase rolling by my side  
No grand return, no perfect line  
Just turn the key, we’ll see what’s still alive  

With quarters clicking in the rain  
And dawn coming pale over the town  
You lean your shoulder into mine  
And neither of us turns around  

[Instrumental Outro]
