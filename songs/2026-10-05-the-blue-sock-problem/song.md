# TITLE

The Blue Sock Problem

# STYLE

Indie pop with bedroom-pop edges, 112 BPM, wistful, buoyant, quietly defiant, intimate close-mic alto vocal, clean electric guitar, warm synth bass, drum machine, glockenspiel, muted piano, verses sparse and chorus wider, homemade cassette grain with crisp modern low end.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
It’s 2:17, the block is empty  
Rain draws lines across the glass  
I carry one last basket down here  
Like it might explain the past  

The apartment’s clean in all the wrong ways  
No jacket hanging by the door  
Your keys are gone, the room keeps ringing  
Like they’re still turning in the lock  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Then behind the dryer window  
Something blue goes round and round  
I take a picture in the humming  
Like I’ve finally found you now  

[Chorus]  
It’s the blue sock problem  
One small thing I can hold  
I make it sound important  
So I don’t have to let you go  
Maybe you’ll come meet me  
Maybe you’ll say you changed  
I keep giving little reasons  
To put your name back in my day  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Verse 2]  
The vending coffee tastes like pennies  
I type, “I think you left this here”  
Then I watch the message waiting  
Like a dare I won’t send near  

The washers blink in tired colors  
Someone’s coat is on a chair  
Outside, a bus rolls past the window  
And doesn’t stop for anyone there  

I turn the sock around my fingers  
Check the heel, the faded seam  
There’s a thread I pulled last winter  
When you were sleeping next to me  

[Pre-Chorus]  
And I know the shape of silence  
I know when a door is closed  
Still I let the dryer answer  
With its little thunderous no  

[Chorus]  
It’s the blue sock problem  
One small thing I can hold  
I make it sound important  
So I don’t have to let you go  
Maybe you’ll come meet me  
Maybe you’ll say my name  
I keep giving little reasons  
To make goodbye take a different shape  

[Bridge]  
It’s mine  
The stupid blue sock is mine  
I bought the pair on Harlow Street  
Before I ever knew your life  

It’s mine  
And so is this long walk home  
I don’t need you to come get it  
I don’t need another phone call  

The text stays there, unopened  
Bright against the morning sky  
I put the sock into my pocket  
And let your number stay behind  

[Final Chorus]  
It was the blue sock problem  
One small thing I could hold  
I made it sound important  
So I wouldn’t have to let you go  
Now the sun comes up between the buildings  
And the rain has lost its weight  
I keep walking with my blue sock  
And I leave your number where it stayed  

It was the blue sock problem  
Nothing more and nothing less  
I thought I needed one last reason  
Turns out I needed to stop inventing them  

[Instrumental Outro]
