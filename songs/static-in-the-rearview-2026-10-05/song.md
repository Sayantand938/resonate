# TITLE

Static in the Rearview

# STYLE

Alternative rock with indie-pop hooks at 142 BPM, restless, bruised, defiant, urgent slightly ragged lead vocal, overdriven electric guitars, melodic electric bass, live drum kit, analog synth, piano, tight verses opening into a wide cathartic chorus, gritty tape saturation and raw room warmth.

# LYRICS

[Instrumental Intro]

[Verse 1]  
The video store is locked from the inside  
Dust on the counter, dead neon sign  
I pull my duffel from the back room floor  
Past the cardboard cutouts and the bolted door  

Outside, the town is dressing up in flame  
Kids on the sidewalks shouting every name  
I find my keys beneath a stack of tapes  
And leave the only place that knew my face  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Then you show up with a cracked cassette  
Saying, “You can’t disappear like this yet”  
You hold it out like proof I owe  
Every street I’m finally letting go  

[Chorus]  
Static in the rearview, fire across the glass  
All the words I swallowed here are coming back at last  
If leaving is a crime, write it on the county line  
I’m not running from my past  
I’m taking back my life  

[Verse 2]  
You say the ones who stayed are still awake  
Still working double shifts for barely pay  
Still meeting by the laundromat at ten  
Still asking where I went, and who I think I am  

I put the tape into the dashboard slot  
It chews for half a second, then it catches hot  
The fireworks turn the windshield red and white  
I miss the turn, then take it anyway tonight  

[Pre-Chorus]  
At the gas station, coffee tastes like tin  
You say, “You’ll call when all this wears you thin”  
I watch the county sign go sliding by  
Your voice gets smaller in the passenger side  

[Chorus]  
Static in the rearview, fire across the glass  
All the words I swallowed here are coming back at last  
If leaving is a crime, write it on the county line  
I’m not running from my past  
I’m taking back my life  

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Bridge]  
And there I am at seventeen  
Talking too loud in somebody’s basement  
Singing into a cheap machine  
Like I could make the whole world listen  

The tape goes thin, then my voice comes through  
“Don’t let them tell you what you’re meant to do”  
I almost turn around beneath the overpass  
But the younger me keeps singing back  

[Final Chorus]  
Static in the rearview, sparks across the glass  
Every mile I drive makes room for what comes next  
Let the locked doors rust, let the old names fade  
I’m not running from my past  
I’m taking back my voice  
Static in the rearview, sunrise turning gray  
No promise waiting for me, but I’m on my way  
If leaving is a crime, write it on the county line  
I’m not running from my past  
I’m taking back my life  

[Instrumental Outro]
